# Changelog

Each version's section below becomes the notes of its GitHub release.

## 0.2.0

- **Edit text in any language:** text you type with **Edit Text** in Russian or
  another language written in Cyrillic or Greek now appears as you typed it,
  instead of question marks. So do curly quotes, dashes and the euro sign (€).
- **Move and resize what you add:** after you edit text or place an image, it
  stays selected, like a signature. Drag it to move it, drag a corner to resize
  it, nudge it with the arrow keys, or press Delete to remove it. You can still
  move it after saving and reopening the file, and it stays part of the page,
  so the text can still be searched.
- **Images:** placing an image in a box no longer blanks out what's under the
  box. Clicking an image you added replaces it in place.
- **Edit Text:** replacement text no longer shrinks when it fits at its
  original size.

## 0.1.2

- **macOS:** PDF Studio now runs on Macs with Apple silicon or Intel
  processors (macOS 13 Ventura or later). Double-clicking a PDF in Finder opens
  it in PDF Studio once you choose it as the default PDF app.
- **Windows:** `PDFStudio.exe` shows its name and version in its file
  properties, and releases can be code-signed.
- **Check for Updates:** **Help → Check for Updates…** shows what's new in the
  latest version. On Windows, **Update** installs it and reopens your
  documents; on a Mac, it downloads the disk image. This is the only time
  PDF Studio connects to the internet, and only when you choose it.

## 0.1.1

- **Search panel:** the search field and options now stay at the top of the
  panel. When nothing is found, the panel says why and what to try: check the
  spelling, turn off *Match case* or *Whole words*, or, for a scanned document,
  that it has no searchable text.
- **Dark theme:** checkboxes are clearly visible, in the search panel and in
  dialogs.

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- **Annotate:** highlight, underline and strike through text; add notes, text
  boxes, ink, shapes and arrows; edit any annotation in the floating inspector.
- **Sign:** draw or import a signature and reuse it; rubber stamps.
- **Edit:** replace text in place (the original text is removed), redact, and
  replace images.
- **Organize:** reorder pages by dragging thumbnails; rotate, delete, duplicate
  and insert pages; extract, split and combine PDFs.
- **Read:** continuous, single-page and two-page views, tabs, search,
  bookmarks, light and dark themes, and password-protected PDFs.
