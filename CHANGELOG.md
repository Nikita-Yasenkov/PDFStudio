# Changelog

Each version's section below becomes the notes of its GitHub release.

## 0.1.1

- **Search panel:** the search field and options now stay at the top of the
  panel. When nothing is found, the panel says why and what to try: check the
  spelling, turn off *Match case* or *Whole words*, or, for a scanned document,
  that it has no searchable text.
- **Dark theme:** checkboxes are clearly visible, in the search panel and in
  dialogs.

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- **Annotate:** highlight, underline and strike through text; add notes, text
  boxes, ink, shapes and arrows; edit any annotation in the floating inspector.
- **Sign:** draw or import a signature and reuse it; rubber stamps.
- **Edit:** replace text in place (the original text is removed), redact, and
  replace images.
- **Organize:** reorder pages by dragging thumbnails; rotate, delete, duplicate
  and insert pages; extract, split and combine PDFs.
- **Read:** continuous, single-page and two-page views, tabs, search,
  bookmarks, light and dark themes, and password-protected PDFs.
